EOB Match

Is my medical bill right?

EOB Match helps you understand your medical bills. It compares the bill from your doctor or hospital with your insurance plan's Explanation of Benefits (EOB), shows how the billed amount turned into what you owe, and tells you what to do if the two don't match.

Frequently asked questions

What is an EOB?

An Explanation of Benefits is a statement from your health plan showing what the provider billed, what the plan allowed and paid, and what you may owe. It is not a bill. Most plans post EOBs on their online member portal or app and also mail them. EOB Match includes a guide to getting and reading yours.

My bill doesn't match my EOB. What should I do?

EOB Match explains the most likely reason and gives you next steps, including what to say when you call the provider's billing office or your plan. A common cause is that the bill was sent before insurance finished processing the claim.

I got a bill but no EOB. Why?

Usually the claim is still being processed, or the provider never sent it to your insurance. The app's "No EOB yet" section walks you through what to do and how long to wait.

Why are my deductible and out-of-pocket totals off?

The app only knows about the bills you've added. If you joined your plan mid-year or had other claims, enter your year-to-date totals from your plan's portal in the Plan section.

Which iPhones get the plain-English explanations?

The plain-English summaries and questions use Apple Intelligence and are available on iPhones that support it. The bill check, verdicts, and dollar amounts work on every iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Is my information private?

Yes. Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and tap Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

Is this medical, legal, or financial advice?

No. EOB Match is an educational tool and is not affiliated with any insurance company or provider. Always confirm details with your plan or provider.
