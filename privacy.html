Privacy Policy

EOB Match · Effective November 8, 2026

The short version: EOB Match does not collect, share, or sell any of your information. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. EOB Match has no account sign-up, no server, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. The developer never receives your bills, Explanations of Benefits, plan documents, or anything you type into the app.

Information stored on your device

To do its job, the app saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • Bills, EOBs, and plan documents you import, and the amounts read from them
  • Your plan details and the year-to-date deductible and out-of-pocket totals you enter

This information is stored using iOS Data Protection, so it is encrypted and unavailable while your iPhone is locked. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

If you choose to scan a document or pick a photo, the app reads the text from that image on your iPhone. Images are not uploaded anywhere. The app only sees the photos you select.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the app can use Apple's on-device model to write plain-English summaries and answer questions. This runs on your iPhone; the app does not send your information to any outside AI service.

Links to other websites

The app may open your health plan's website in your browser when you ask it to. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and tap Erase all data, or delete the app. Either removes everything the app stored.

Children

EOB Match is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
