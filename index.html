Is my medical bill right?

EOB Match puts your provider's bill next to your insurance plan's Explanation of Benefits and shows you, in plain English, why you owe what you owe — and what to do if something doesn't add up.

How it works

Three steps, a few minutes, no jargon.

1

Add your bill

Import a PDF, scan the paper bill with your camera, or pick a photo.

2

Add your EOB

Not sure where to find it? The app shows you how to get it from your plan and how to read it.

3

Get a clear answer

See the verdict, how the billed amount became what you owe, and your next steps.

Built around your plan

Most bills are correct. The confusing part is your share. EOB Match explains it.

Your plan's numbers

Import your plan's Summary of Benefits and Coverage so explanations use your real copays, coinsurance and deductible.

Year-to-date totals

Track how much of your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum you've met, including family totals.

Call scripts

Know exactly what to say to the billing office or your plan when something doesn't match.

Plain-English answers

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, ask follow-up questions. The math always comes from the app's own calculations, never guessed by AI.

Private by design

Your bills and EOBs never leave your iPhone. They're stored encrypted on your device, and you can erase them any time.
